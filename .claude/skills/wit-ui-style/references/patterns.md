# Page patterns

## Dashboard (admin, the plant or operations view)
1. View row `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-2`: `PillTabs` view switch (Control tower | Shift view), persisted per viewer, and a context caption `hidden text-xs text-muted md:block` ("Refinery Jakarta · updated 09:41").
2. Attention card, `Card className="p-4"` labelled by its title: `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-3` with the title `font-semibold` "Attention required" over a `text-xs text-muted` helper ("Open the queue that needs action first."), and a tile grid `grid w-full grid-cols-2 gap-2 sm:grid-cols-3 xl:w-auto` (`xl:grid-cols-6` on a full-width control page). Show only queues with a count above zero, sorted by count. Each tile is a Link to its pre-filtered list (`/orders?view=at-risk`): `flex min-w-0 items-center gap-3 rounded-2xl px-3 py-2.5 transition-colors` + focus ring, `bg-accent-soft text-accent-strong hover:bg-accent-soft/70` when the queue is urgent, `bg-surface text-foreground hover:bg-surface-2` otherwise; content: an icon (`[&_svg]:size-4`), the count `block text-lg font-bold leading-none tabular-nums` and the label `block truncate text-[11px] font-semibold`. With nothing open: `col-span-2 py-3 text-sm text-muted` "No active issues."
3. Hero grid `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 xl:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1.5fr)_minmax(0,1fr)]`:
   - Left: the featured record on an ink card `flex flex-col p-5`. Pick it by urgency: the at-risk record first, else the running one that ends soonest. Kicker ("At risk" or "Featured order"), `mt-1 truncate text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight` title, a `text-sm text-on-ink-muted` meta line (`code · line · revision`), the status badge top right; the giant number in `mt-6 flex items-end gap-2 leading-none`: `text-6xl font-bold tracking-tight tabular-nums` with a `pb-1 text-sm font-semibold text-on-ink-muted` caption; step chips `mt-4 flex flex-wrap gap-2` of links `inline-flex h-8 items-center gap-1.5 rounded-full border px-3 text-xs font-semibold` (running `border-accent-strong bg-accent-strong text-white`, done `border-white/10 bg-white/10 text-white`, upcoming `border-white/10 text-on-ink-muted hover:bg-white/10`); a latest-event panel `mt-4 rounded-2xl bg-white/5 p-3 text-sm`; a footer `mt-auto flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-2 pt-5` with the progress text and an `onInk` "Open order". With no record: the ink card holds an EmptyState.
   - Right: `grid grid-cols-1 gap-3 sm:gap-4` with the accent card for the one urgent metric (number, context, white progress bar, `onInk` "Open list") above `grid grid-cols-2 gap-3 sm:gap-4` of two StatCards (the ink primary and an info one).
4. Status overview: one StatCard per status in `grid grid-cols-2 gap-3 sm:gap-4 md:grid-cols-3 xl:grid-cols-6`, each in its status tone, plus a "Delayed" card (`danger` above zero, `success` at zero).
5. Lists and analysis `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 md:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_minmax(0,1fr)_20rem]`: two list cards (CardTitle with a CountBadge, outline sm "View all" as the header action, 5 nested rows with a sequence tile and a phone status line, `EmptyState compact` when empty) and an analysis card (`md:col-span-2 xl:col-span-1`) with a Donut and a ghost full-width "Open WIP view" link.
6. The second view (Shift view): four StatCards, a SegmentBar card with its legend, and a log card of history rows.

Role-aware home: roles without the plant dashboard land on their own home. `PageHeader` "<Role> home", "Your next actions at <site>.", and a primary link to the role's main queue ("Open dispatch"); one `Card className="p-5"` with an `text-lg font-semibold` heading ("Needs dispatch") and `mt-4 grid gap-2 sm:grid-cols-2` of up to six work items `rounded-2xl bg-surface-2 p-4 hover:bg-surface` (a `block text-sm font-semibold` title over a `mt-1 block text-xs text-muted` detail); an `EmptyState compact` when nothing waits.

CMMS variant (maintenance): the page has an `sr-only` h1 and opens with an info Banner when drafts wait for the viewer's approval ("3 work orders wait for your approval" + primary sm "Review"). The attention card keeps four fixed links (P1 work, Overdue, Waiting, Approvals) in `grid w-full grid-cols-2 gap-2 sm:w-auto sm:grid-cols-4` with `min-w-32` tiles, so zero counts stay visible and read as good news. The ink hero features the asset that is down right now (a kicker with a pulsing accent dot, "Production stopped for 2.4 h" at `text-6xl`, the linked work order as the accent chip, a latest-event link `rounded-2xl bg-white/5 p-3 hover:bg-white/10` with ArrowUpRight, `onInk` "Asset passport" beside a primary sm "Open work order"), else the worst offender of the quarter. Its right column drops the accent card for two trend stat cards (`grid grid-cols-2 gap-3 sm:gap-4`, a Sparkline or BarStrip at `mt-auto pt-3`). A summary strip card follows (`grid grid-cols-1 items-center gap-5 p-5 sm:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1.4fr)_minmax(0,1fr)_minmax(0,1fr)_auto]`: active work as a SegmentBar with its legend, two metrics with IconTiles, an outline "View backlog"), then three StatCards in the phone snap row (`flex snap-x snap-mandatory gap-3 overflow-x-auto no-scrollbar [&>*]:min-w-[72%] [&>*]:snap-start sm:grid sm:grid-cols-3 sm:gap-4`), then two list cards whose header action is an inline NativeSelect (team, period) and a work-mix Donut card with a `dl` legend. The Reliability view has four StatCards and a `lg:grid-cols-2` of BarList cards (the worst row in accent), repeat-failure rows with date pills and an "RCA" or "Start RCA" button, and open analyses with a ProgressBar each.

## List page (the default screen)
1. `PageHeader`: title, a one-sentence description of what the screen is for, actions = pill search (`min-w-0 flex-1 sm:w-72 sm:flex-none`) and the primary "New X" when the user may create. Live boards add `<p className="text-xs text-muted">Updated 09:41</p>`; a view switch with counts (`Needs assignment (8)`) goes here as PillTabs.
2. Scoping banner when a deep link narrows the page: `Banner tone="info"` (`warning` when the linked record is missing) with an outline sm "Show all orders" action.
3. Stat tiles `grid grid-cols-2 gap-3 sm:gap-4 xl:grid-cols-4` (up to `xl:grid-cols-5`). Each applies its filter or deep link on click; tone follows the count; coverage reads as a unit (`value={12} unit="of 40"`); an odd last tile gets `col-span-2 xl:col-span-1`.
4. Filter bar `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-2`: a `ChipRow className="max-w-full"` of `Chip variant="filter"` with counts ("All" first, a second click on the active chip clears it) and data-backed filters as inline pickers (`variant="inline" clearable placeholder="Any shift" aria-label="Filter by shift"`).
5. `DataTable` on the canvas, without a card: `getRowKey`, `onRowClick` to the detail route, `resetPageKey`, `{...useTableHistory()}`, and an `EmptyState compact` as `empty`.

State: search and filters in `useHistoryState`, filters that other pages link to in the URL (`?view=`, replace), a view choice the viewer keeps across sessions in storage. A Cards / Table toggle is PillTabs persisted under `<ns>.admin.<page>.view`; the card grid is `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 md:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-3` of cards with `role="link" tabIndex={0}` and `hover:bg-surface-2`, no lift.

Card form (CMMS work orders, requests): when a list has several views, the views, the filters and the table live in one Card under the PageHeader.
1. `flex flex-wrap items-end gap-3 px-5 pt-3`: UnderlineTabs of the views with counts (Active · Overdue · To verify and close · Closed · All) in `?view=` (replace; the default view deletes the param), and on phones an outline sm "Filters · N" button (`mb-2`).
2. From `md`: a filter row `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 border-b border-border px-5 py-3` of default Chips (type, priority), inline clearable pickers with `aria-label` (team, assignee) and a ghost sm "Clear N filters". On phones the same controls stack in a bottom Sheet whose footer holds outline "Clear" and primary "Show N results".
3. The DataTable, with `resetPageKey` joining the view, the query and the filters, `{...useTableHistory()}`, urgent rows tinted, and an empty state that tells "No work orders match" (Clear filters) from "Nothing here" (where records come from).
A caption under the card names the default order ("Sorted by urgency: overdue first, then priority and due date. Click a column header to sort."). The default sort differs per view: urgency for open work, newest first for closed work.

## Detail page
1. Action bar `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-2`: BackButton (fallback: the list) on the left; on the right the next lifecycle step as the primary ("Release", "Approve", "Move to containment" with a trailing ArrowRight), other lifecycle steps as outline buttons, and last the ActionMenu (outline icon button, MoreHorizontal, titled with the record code) for edit, copy, cancel and a destructive delete. Only actions the user may take in the record's current state render.
2. Blocker: `Card className="p-4 text-sm"` with a bold lead-in ("Cannot complete yet:" / "Before closing:") when a visible action waits on something, or a Banner.
3. Ink hero `Card variant="ink" className="p-5"`:
   - Head `flex flex-wrap items-start justify-between gap-3`: an eyebrow `text-xs font-mono text-on-ink-muted` (`code · rev`), `h1 mt-1 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight` (`font-mono` when the title is a code), a context line `mt-1 flex flex-wrap items-center gap-x-2 text-sm text-on-ink-muted` whose code links are `font-mono text-xs font-semibold text-on-ink hover:text-accent`, and badges (or a `PillTabs size="sm"` window) on the right.
   - Metrics `mt-5 grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 sm:grid-cols-4` (`grid-cols-3 sm:grid-cols-5` for five) of metric-on-ink cells; units sit at the bottom beside hero numbers.
   - A definition line `mt-4 text-xs text-on-ink-muted` when a number needs one ("Finished good counts only output from the final operation.").
   - Progress `mt-4`: labels `mb-1.5 flex items-center justify-between text-xs text-on-ink-muted` over a `h-1.5 rounded-full bg-white/15` track with a `bg-white` fill (`bg-accent` when over the limit).
   - Status notes `mt-3 rounded-2xl px-3 py-2 text-sm`: `bg-accent/20` for risk and holds, `bg-warning/20` for paused, `bg-white/10` for locked records and read-only reminders.
   - Analysis records add a summary `mt-5 text-sm leading-relaxed text-on-ink-muted` and an evidence row `mt-4 flex flex-wrap items-center gap-x-4 gap-y-1 text-xs` of mono record links.
4. Lifecycle: `Card className="p-5"` holding Steps from the status flow; a rejection marks the rest `skipped` and appends a current "Rejected" step.
5. `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 lg:grid-cols-2` of KeyValue cards (`bare` inside CardContent, a ghost sm header link to a related page). Empty values read "None", "Not yet" or "Unassigned"; date ranges read `start → end` (`→ open` or "running" when unfinished).
6. Related cards: nested rows (the header action is an outline sm link to the related board), requirement rows with meters (`rounded-2xl bg-surface-2 p-3`: name and badge, ProgressBar `mt-2`, caption `mt-1 text-[11px] text-muted`), check lists, and SplitStats under a list (`mt-5`).
7. History card of history rows, newest first.
8. Dialogs last: the entity dialog for edit and every ConfirmDialog.

A device or asset with sensors adds a **Live points** card after its KeyValue cards: a CardTitle with a muted "Each sensor against the limits set for it." over `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 md:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-3` of gauge cards (components → Gauges). Sensor rows elsewhere print their limits in words ("Limits: 18 to 28 °C · 30 to 60 %RH").

Not found: a Card holding an EmptyState ("Order not found", "It may have been removed or belongs to another site.") with a BackButton action. A record that belongs to another site the user can open offers a primary "Switch to <site>" instead (CMMS).

Long record variant (CMMS work order, a job card with many sections): no ink hero.
1. BackButton, then a header `flex flex-wrap items-start justify-between gap-4`: on the left a line of the mono code (`font-mono text-xs font-medium text-muted`) and the type, priority and status badges, the `h1 mt-2 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight` title and a context line (the asset link, the description in `text-body/80`); on the right `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-2 print:hidden` with the outline secondaries (Reject, Pause), **one primary chosen from the record's state** (Approve, else Start work, Assign, Complete, Resume, Verify, Close) and a `Button variant="card" size="icon"` `…` ActionMenu (Edit details, Assign, Schedule, Confirm safety, Reopen, Print job card, then the destructive Cancel).
2. Steps straight under the header with the time of each passed step as its hint; optional states (waiting, verified) appear only when the record passes through them, and a cancelled record shows a muted "Cancelled" badge instead.
3. A Banner stack `mb-4 space-y-3 empty:hidden`: danger "Production is stopped on this asset", warning "Past due since …", danger repeat failure with an outline link to its analysis, info "still covered by warranty".
4. A sticky section nav `sticky top-0 z-20 mb-4 overflow-x-auto bg-surface/95 py-2 backdrop-blur no-scrollbar print:hidden` holding a pill track `inline-flex min-w-max gap-1 rounded-full bg-card p-1 shadow-card` of anchor links `rounded-full px-4 py-2 text-sm font-semibold text-muted hover:bg-surface hover:text-foreground` (Overview · Execution · Resources · Findings · Activity); each section takes `scroll-mt-16` and an `aria-label`.
5. `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 xl:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_340px]`: the main column `min-w-0 space-y-4` (checklist, labour and parts as a `2xl:grid-cols-2` pair, tools, attachments, failure coding, activity timeline), the aside `min-w-0 space-y-4` of side cards whose titles are `text-[13px] font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.4px]` (Details as a bare KeyValue at `labelWidth="md"`, Safety, Cost with a totals list, Approval).
6. Every action opens its dialog from one `pending` state; each success dispatches, toasts and clears it.
A print-only caption opens the page ("Job card · site · printed …"; layouts → Print).

Tabbed passport variant (CMMS asset): BackButton → a header with an `IconTile size="lg"` identity tile (`bg-card shadow-card`, danger tone while the asset is down), the mono code with status and criticality badges, the `h1`, a MapPin location line and the parent link, and actions (outline "Report problem", primary "New work order", outline "Edit", an outline `…` menu with status changes that carry descriptions and a guarded delete) → `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 xl:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_340px]`: the main column holds an ink hero of the key numbers (a kicker "Reliability · last 90 days", a one-line headline, an accent repeat-failure chip beside a `bg-white/10` link chip to its analysis, `grid grid-cols-2 gap-3 sm:grid-cols-4` of metrics on `rounded-2xl bg-white/5 p-4`) above a Card whose UnderlineTabs (`px-5 pt-2`, counts, the tab in `?tab=`) switch the panel in `p-5`; the aside is `grid grid-cols-1 content-start gap-4 md:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-1` of side cards (QR label with Print label, warranty, calibration, PM schedules, responsible people). Blocks inside a panel open with a section title: `h3 flex items-center gap-2 text-sm font-semibold` + a count pill `rounded-full bg-surface px-1.5 py-px text-[11px] font-bold tabular-nums` + an action on the right.

## Master data
Console (many entities): `/master-data/:domain` → `PageHeader` with the domain's description → `PillTabs` of the domains → `<Panel key={domain} />`. A panel is `UnderlineTabs` (the active tab in `?tab=`, replace) with one entity table per tab:
- `EntityTable`: a Card whose `CardHeader` holds the title, a description and, as the action, a pill search `w-full sm:w-56` and the Add button; a DataTable where a row click opens edit; an `_actions` column (`width: '5.5rem'`) with ghost `icon-sm` Edit and Remove, Remove disabled with `title={reason}` when other records reference the row; a ConfirmDialog "Remove {name}?".
- `EntityDialog` driven by a `Field<T>` union (`text | number | select | combobox | multi | switch | custom`, each with `required`, `hint`, `span: 2`, `mono`, `readOnly`), an automatic "Code is required." and a case-insensitive code-uniqueness check, plus a custom `validate(draft)`. One `useEditor<T>()` hook holds `editing` and `removing` for each tab.
- Without the permission, the table renders with no Add, no actions column and no row edit.
Single entity: `PageHeader` (search `w-72` + "Add X") → the table → the entity dialog → ConfirmDialog. A small record set (types, categories) may use a card grid `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 md:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-3` whose cards carry an icon tile, a count badge and ghost icon actions.
CMMS form (one page of lists): `PageHeader` (a muted "View only" badge with a Lock icon for roles that may not edit) → UnderlineTabs with a count per list, in `?tab=` and outside any card (`mb-4`) → one Card per tab. The card's header `flex flex-wrap items-start justify-between gap-3 p-5` pairs an `h2 text-base font-semibold` title and a "used in" line that names where the list shows up ("Job plans ask for a skill level, and the skill matrix and assignment pickers show who holds it") with a soft rounded search (`w-full sm:w-60`) and the primary "Add X". Row actions are ghost `icon-sm` buttons with tooltips from `md` and one `…` ActionMenu on phones (delete after a separator). The delete confirm refuses while other records use the row ("Used by 12 work orders. It stays while those records point at it. Edit it instead if the name is wrong."). Each tab keeps its own search in history state (`skills.query`), so switching tabs keeps it, and a shared `useEditor()` holds the dialog and removal targets so they survive the closing animation.

## Revisioned documents (BOM, routing, specification, work instruction)
- Lifecycle draft → review → released → obsolete. Releasing a revision makes the previous released one obsolete. Released data is read-only; it changes only through "Copy as new draft" or a change request (ECO), and the hero says so in a `bg-white/10` note.
- One shell component renders the hero, Steps, the document body and a "Used by" card at the end, so every document type behaves the same.
- Lists mark released documents with the muted Lock badge ("Released").
- Revision history uses the revision timeline (components). A compare view shows "From" and "To" panels `rounded-2xl bg-surface-2 px-4 py-3`, each with `KeyValue bare labelWidth="sm"`; affected documents get an accent "Affected" badge and the rest a muted "Unchanged".
- A change request links out with `?new=1&product=<id>` so the create dialog opens prefilled.

## Approval queues
Stacked cards, one per queue kind; a card renders nothing at zero and shows a CountBadge in its title. Each row is a nested row with a verb button ("Release", "Approve"). One `pending: { kind, id } | null` state mounts the matching ConfirmDialog (`key={pending.id} open`). Approve and reject share the dialog; reject requires a note.
Decision cards (CMMS, when each item needs its context to decide): a card per pending record in a grid. CardHeader: a line of the mono code link with priority and type badges, `CardTitle text-lg leading-snug` title, and the approval level as the header action (`ink` Badge for the top level, `outline` for the lower one). Content `flex flex-1 flex-col gap-4`: the asset link with its icon tile, the requester chip with "requested 2 h ago" beside the due time (`font-semibold text-accent` once late), a reason strip `flex items-start gap-2 rounded-2xl bg-surface-2 px-3 py-2.5 text-sm` with a warning icon, a Kicker "Estimated cost" over a `divide-y rounded-2xl bg-surface-2 px-4` line list with its total (and a muted note on how far it passes the threshold), and a footer `mt-auto space-y-2 border-t border-border pt-4` with primary Approve, outline Reject and a ghost sm "Open work order" at `ml-auto`. A viewer who may approve but not at this level sees the buttons disabled and a `text-xs text-muted` line that names who decides ("Rina Kartika can approve this."): the level is a state of the record, so the card stays visible. The decision dialog takes a note (required to reject) and the toast says what happens next ("Budi and Andi can start the work").

## Boards (dispatch, production control)
Dispatch board: machine columns scroll sideways at every width.
- Scroller `no-scrollbar flex gap-4 overflow-x-auto pb-2`; column card `flex w-72 min-w-72 shrink-0 flex-col p-3`, dimmed with `opacity-80` and an accent Lock icon when the machine is blocked; column header with the name and a count pill `shrink-0 rounded-full bg-surface px-2 py-0.5 text-[11px] font-bold tabular-nums`; empty slot `rounded-2xl bg-surface-2 px-3 py-6 text-center text-xs text-muted`.
- Card `div role="link" tabIndex={0}` `cursor-pointer rounded-2xl bg-surface-2 p-3 transition-colors hover:bg-surface`: code, operation, planned times, an `AvatarStack size="xs" max={3}` and a ghost `icon-sm` ActionMenu. A `?wo=` link gives its card `ring-2 ring-accent` and scrolls it to the centre.
- One hook supplies the actions (`useWoActions()` returns `items(record)`, `open(kind, record)` and `dialogs`), mounted once per page, so the board, the table and the detail page share menus and dialogs.
- Phones: the "Needs assignment" list stacks rows `flex flex-col gap-3 rounded-2xl bg-surface-2 p-3 sm:flex-row sm:items-center`.
Production control: attention tiles, machine chips `inline-flex h-9 items-center gap-2 rounded-full bg-surface-2 pl-3 pr-3.5 text-xs font-semibold transition-colors hover:bg-surface` with a StatusDot (pulsing while the machine runs), machine cards `w-full rounded-card bg-card p-4 text-left shadow-card` with an alarm line `mt-2 text-xs font-semibold text-accent`, a mono order and work-order line, and `Sparkline height={28}` (`tone="accent"` when down). A group-by switch sits under the CardTitle as `PillTabs size="sm" className="mt-1"`.

## Timelines (Gantt, machine schedule)
- A fixed scale inside `overflow-x-auto` at every width (192px per day, 44px rows, a 176px label column, ticks at 06, 12 and 18, a 15-minute drag snap), so phones scroll the same chart. A label column stays pinned with `sticky left-0 z-10 flex shrink-0 flex-col justify-center bg-card px-3`. A header row cannot stick to the top inside the `overflow-x-auto` box, because that box becomes its scroll container: keep the chart short or repeat the scale.
- Group band `flex items-center bg-surface-2 px-3 py-1 text-[11px] font-semibold uppercase tracking-wider text-muted`; day lines `absolute inset-y-0 border-l border-border`; hour lines `absolute inset-y-0 border-l border-dashed border-border/60`; tick labels `absolute top-0 text-[10px] text-muted tabular-nums`.
- Bar `absolute top-2 flex h-7 min-w-1.5 select-none items-center overflow-hidden rounded-lg px-2 text-[11px] font-semibold shadow-card transition-shadow` + focus ring. Timeline bars are chart marks, so they take solid fills: planned `bg-ink`, in progress `bg-info`, paused `bg-warning`, done `bg-success`, cancelled `bg-silver/60`, and critical `bg-accent` whatever its status. Selected `ring-2 ring-accent ring-offset-1 ring-offset-card`, dragging `z-20 opacity-90`, native `title` as the tooltip.
- Now line `pointer-events-none absolute bottom-0 top-9 z-10 w-px bg-accent` with a pill `absolute -left-4 -top-4 rounded-full bg-accent px-1.5 text-[10px] font-bold text-white` ("now").
- The chart sits in `Card className="p-4"` under a badge strip `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-2 text-sm`, with a caption `mt-2 text-[11px] text-muted` that explains the scale and the gestures.

## Calendar (month, week, agenda; CMMS)
Planned work on a calendar in a Card, with PillTabs for Month · Week · Agenda and previous / today / next controls in the header.
- Month: `grid grid-cols-7`, weekday headers `px-2 py-2 text-[11px] font-semibold uppercase tracking-wide text-muted`, day cells with a date button that opens the day and up to three entries plus a `rounded-full px-2 py-0.5 text-[11px] font-semibold text-muted hover:bg-surface` "+N more".
- Week: `grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-7`, so a phone reads the week as a list of days with "N items" or "Nothing planned"; columns `md:min-h-80`.
- Agenda: `divide-y` day sections (`px-4 py-3`, the date as a link to the day, a muted count) of entry rows.
- An entry chip has three shapes: month `flex h-6 w-full items-center gap-1 rounded-full px-2 text-[11px] font-medium` (bold code + label, truncated), week `flex w-full flex-col gap-0.5 rounded-xl px-2 py-1.5 text-xs` (time and code over a `line-clamp-2` label), agenda `flex w-full items-center gap-3 rounded-2xl px-3 py-2.5 text-sm` (a `w-11` time column). Tones come from the work type as soft tints, an overdue entry adds a `size-1.5` dot, hover is `hover:brightness-95`, and the `aria-label` spells out the entry.
- Drag an entry to another day to reschedule it (`cursor-grab`, `active:cursor-grabbing`), then confirm and toast.
- Clicking an entry opens a quick look, a `w-[22rem] max-w-[calc(100vw-1.5rem)] p-4` Popover from `md` and a bottom sheet on phones, with the details and a link to the record's page.
- A legend row closes the card: `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-x-3 gap-y-2 border-t border-border px-5 py-3` of tone chips `inline-flex h-6 items-center gap-1.5 rounded-full px-2.5 text-[11px] font-semibold`, the overdue dot, and a hint at `md:ml-auto` ("Drag to reschedule").

## Record-picker pages (trace a lot, a serial, an order's genealogy)
- The picker sits in the PageHeader actions at `min-w-0 flex-1 sm:w-80 sm:flex-none`; the selection lives in the URL (`?id=`, `?mo=`, replace). Before a pick, a Card holds an EmptyState with an icon that says what to pick.
- Side-list layout `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 xl:grid-cols-[20rem_minmax(0,1fr)]`: the list card `max-h-[32rem] space-y-1 overflow-y-auto p-2`, capped at 60 rows with a note, rows `flex w-full items-center gap-3 rounded-2xl p-3 text-left transition-colors hover:bg-surface-2`, the selected row filled.
- Search results as chips `inline-flex h-8 items-center gap-2 rounded-full bg-surface-2 px-3 font-mono text-xs font-semibold hover:bg-surface`.
- The trace itself: tree rows and a flow chain (components).
- On phones the list comes first; after a pick, scroll the detail into view.

## Copy, state and permission rules
- Copy is plain and specific. Errors are full-sentence instructions ("Choose the product."). Meta joins with ` · `. Empty values read "None", "Not yet" or "Unassigned". Confirm titles are questions naming the code. Empty states name what is missing or the good outcome, then say when records arrive. No em dashes, no exclamation marks.
- Hide what the role cannot do (`can(permission)` from the auth context); disable only when the record's state blocks the action, and say why with a `title`, an ActionMenu item description ("Referenced by 2 revisions and 1 order"), a Banner or a blocker note. One `xBlocker(state, record): string | null` function feeds both the reducer's refusal and the UI's reason. An approval above the viewer's level counts as a state of the record: CMMS keeps the card visible with its buttons disabled and a line naming who decides.
- Deep links: `?view=`, `?tab=`, `?id=`, `?wo=`, `?machine=` (set with `replace: true`, deleted at their default), `?new=1` and `?receive=1` open a create dialog, `?new=1&product=<id>` prefills it; old parameter shapes redirect with `<Navigate replace>`. After a create opened through `?new=1`, navigate to the new record with `replace`, so Back does not reopen the form (CMMS).
- Clocks: `useNow(30_000)` on live pages, `useNow(60_000)` elsewhere; relative times through the shared formatter.
- Toast every change and say what it caused.
- Never edit released or posted records in place: copy, reverse or raise a change.
- Page descriptions say what the reader can do there, in their words ("Track new alerts, active responses, resolved incidents, and verification history"). Plumbing (API, backend, endpoint paths) stays off every screen except the integration console, and a button names its effect for the reader ("Check connection").
- List titles and nav labels are plural nouns ("Devices", "Contact Persons"). Loading lines name the thing ("Loading your outlets…"); a load error names what failed and offers "Try again".
- Credits name the client's product first and the vendor second ("Outlet environment monitoring · built by WIT.ID").
- A feature the rollout has not reached stays visible and disabled with a hint that says it comes later (components → Unavailable options); a missing permission still hides the action.

## Checklist before finishing a screen
- [ ] One accent element per region; the second emphasis is ink. Solid accent behind small white text uses `accent-strong`.
- [ ] A client palette is sampled from the logo and mapped per branding.md, and its contrast gate passes; no hex or rgb literal sits in a class or a component (charts, maps and SVG read `BRAND`).
- [ ] An API-backed page waits behind a skeleton, fails into a Try again row, and shows success only after the server confirms.
- [ ] Cards are `rounded-card bg-card shadow-card` with no borders; the canvas is `surface`; nested content sits in `rounded-2xl bg-surface-2` rows that hover to `bg-surface`. A list page's main table follows the product's one list form (on the canvas, or in one card with its view tabs and filters); section tables sit in cards.
- [ ] Controls are pills; icons are lucide at `size-4` or `size-5`; icon tiles are soft tints except the one ink or accent tile.
- [ ] Numbers are big and tabular with the unit muted beside them (top-aligned in stat cards and readings, bottom-aligned beside hero numbers); identifiers are mono.
- [ ] Every data-backed dropdown is a searchable combobox; plain selects only for fixed enums of six or fewer.
- [ ] Forms show errors after the first submit; required marks, hints and defaults are visible; submit names the verb.
- [ ] Every mutation ends in a toast; every blocked action says why; actions the role cannot take are hidden.
- [ ] Empty states have a title, a description and, where possible, the next action; every button does something real.
- [ ] Lists keep search, filters and table position across Back; filters that other pages link to live in the URL.
- [ ] Hover is `surface` (white-10 on dark), press is scale 0.98, focus is the accent ring; motion stays at fades, slides and a pulse on live states.
- [ ] Mobile: titles end with the accent period, the tab bar hides on detail screens, lists are tabbed and lazy-loaded 6 at a time, touch targets are at least 44px, the primary action sits in the sticky bar.
- [ ] Phones: every menu, picker and filter opens as a bottom sheet with a grab handle and safe-area padding.
- [ ] Responsive: every breakpoint-only grid has a `grid-cols-1` base and `minmax(0, …)` tracks; header and card rows wrap; checked at 375 and 768 with `main.scrollWidth === main.clientWidth`.
- [ ] Charts: an `ariaLabel`, a `format`, one highlighted value, plans as reference lines, and a "No data" state.
- [ ] Headings: one `h1` per page (`sr-only` when the design shows none), card titles one level below, no skipped level; a skip link reaches `main`; icon-only buttons and unlabelled pickers carry an `aria-label`.
- [ ] Record pages print as documents: the chrome hidden, cards whole, a caption with the print time (layouts → Print); check print preview.
